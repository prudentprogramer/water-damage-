Check your ZIP code before calling

Coverage depends on the approved service-area list and provider availability. Use this checker as a starting point.

Why we check ZIP codes

Water-damage services are not available through every provider in every ZIP code. A ZIP-code check helps avoid implying that a service is available everywhere.

What a result means

When an approved ZIP list is configured, a matching ZIP means only that the code appears on the current list. It does not guarantee a provider will take the job, provide a particular service, arrive within a particular time or charge a particular price.

If your ZIP is not listed

Coverage can change, and a missing ZIP may reflect the current list rather than a permanent service limitation. Call the connection line to ask about your area.

No made-up city pages

We do not publish pages claiming a local office, local technicians or guaranteed service in a city unless those details are genuinely supported. Location pages should be added only when they provide useful, accurate information.
