A clear way to explore local water-damage help

HomeWaterConnect is a connection-focused service. We help homeowners start a conversation about service options; independent providers decide whether they can take a job and what work may be appropriate.

1. Check your ZIP code

Enter a five-digit US ZIP code in the coverage checker. The tool can only reflect the approved list that has been configured for this site. If the list is not available or your ZIP is not shown, call to ask about coverage.

2. Call the connection line

Call +1 (855) 321-3117 and explain the kind of water issue you are dealing with and where the property is located. Do not share sensitive personal or financial information unless you understand why it is needed and who will receive it.

3. Ask about provider availability

A connection may depend on your ZIP code, the service needed and participating provider availability. A call or ZIP-code match does not guarantee a provider, an appointment, a particular response time or a specific result.

4. Review the provider before authorizing work

Providers are independent. Ask for the business name, relevant license and insurance details, a written scope and estimate, payment terms and any exclusions. Verify credentials and requirements for your state before hiring.

Our role and compensation

HomeWaterConnect is not the contractor performing the restoration work. The site may receive compensation for qualifying calls or connections through marketing partners. That compensation does not guarantee service quality, availability, coverage or a particular outcome.
