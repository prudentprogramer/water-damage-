Frequently asked questions

Before you call

A few things to know about coverage, independent providers and the connection process.

Does HomeWaterConnect perform water-damage restoration?

No. HomeWaterConnect is a connection service. Contractors and providers are independent and are responsible for their own work, credentials, prices and service terms.

Does a matching ZIP code guarantee help?

No. A match only indicates that the ZIP code appears on the configured coverage list. Provider participation, service needs and availability must be confirmed directly.

Will HomeWaterConnect quote a repair price?

No. The independent provider should explain its own scope and pricing. Ask for a written estimate and clarify exclusions, extra charges and payment terms before authorizing work.

Can I rely on this site for an emergency response?

No. This site is not an emergency response service and does not promise a particular arrival time. If there is immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency service, utility or qualified professional.

How can I verify a provider?

Ask for the provider’s legal business name, relevant license and insurance details, a written scope, a clear estimate and references where appropriate. Verify the requirements that apply in your state.
